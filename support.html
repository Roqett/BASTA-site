Support

BASTA! is made by one person. If something is wrong, the fastest way to get it fixed is to say what happened.

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Getting in touch

Email roqett@proton.me. Please say which phone you are on (iPhone SE, iPhone 17, iPad — the layout differs), and what you were doing when it went wrong.

There is no ticket system and no chat bot. There is a person.

Getting the app

BASTA! is in testing on iOS 17 and later. To join the TestFlight list, email the address above with the subject TestFlight and the email address you want the invitation sent to.

How the app works, in short

  • Three draws a day. Each lens has its own allowance once you have bought the app; during the trial one draw a day, on any lens.
  • Looking is free. You can read the day’s reading for any card as often as you like, and it never uses a draw.
  • Fresh cards at midnight. The deck is new again at local midnight, wherever you are.
  • The app needs the internet once, to fetch random numbers from the ANU quantum generator. If it cannot, it says so and uses your device’s own secure generator instead. Nothing else ever leaves your phone.

Common questions

Why does it say “device · secure random”?

That is the app telling you the truth about where the randomness for your reading came from. It tried the ANU quantum generator and could not reach it, so it used the secure generator built into your phone. Both are fine; the app simply does not pretend.

Can I get my three draws back?

They come back at local midnight. If you are testing the app, there is a testing panel at the bottom of the settings drawer with a “Refill now” button.

Does the app need an account?

No, and it never will. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to sign in with. See the privacy policy.

Do the readings change every time?

No. Every reading is written by a person and ships inside the app, so the same card and the same lens always say the same thing. The randomness is in which card you draw, not in what it says.

The reading is being spoken. Can I stop it?

Yes — the button under the card becomes “stop” while it is speaking. You can also choose whether the reading is spoken as it arrives or only when you press the button, in the settings drawer.
