Privacy policy

BASTA! collects no personal data. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics, so there is nothing for it to collect. This policy says what that means in practice, in full.

Last updated 20 October 2026.

1. What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no email address, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, no name, no location, no contacts, no photos, no crash reports sent to us, and no analytics of any kind.

Nothing you do in the app — which card you draw, which lens you read, which settings you change — is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

2. The one network call

BASTA! makes exactly one kind of network request, and it is made so that a draw cannot be predicted:

A request to the Australian National University’s Quantum Random Number Generator (the ANU QRNG) for a block of random bytes. The request carries no personal data and no device information of any kind — it asks a public generator for random numbers, and that is all.

The app tells you when it does this. The line of text at the foot of the screen names the source of the entropy for the reading in front of you — the ANU QRNG or your device’s own secure generator — so the promise is visible, not buried in a policy.

If the ANU request fails or is unavailable, the app uses the secure random generator built into your device. No data is sent in that case either.

3. What stays on your device

Everything else the app remembers, it remembers on your device only, in the app’s own private storage:

  • the readings you have drawn, so that a reading you have already had can be found again;
  • your allowance for the day, so the app knows how many draws are left;
  • the settings you change, such as your appearance and how you would like the reading delivered.

This data is never uploaded, synced, shared or backed up anywhere. It is not readable by us, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Because there is no account, it cannot be used to identify you, and it cannot be transferred to another device.

4. Sharing and selling

BASTA! does not share, sell, rent or trade personal data. It has no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting service and no attribution SDKs. There is no company with access to your data, because there is no data.

5. Purchases

BASTA! makes no in-app purchases. If a paid version is offered in future, it will be a one-off purchase made through the App Store, and no purchase details will reach us.

6. Children

BASTA! collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children either. It is not directed at children.

7. This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages set no cookies, run no analytics, embed no fonts, images or scripts from any other company, and make no request to any third party. The only way this page knows you exist is the ordinary, unavoidable web-server access log.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app, go to roqett@proton.me. If you would like the data the app holds on your device removed, deleting the app removes it — there is nothing else to ask for.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the app’s own privacy disclosure changes with it too. An app that behaves differently from this page would be a bug, and we would rather fix it than quietly rewrite the page.
