BASTA!

One card. Three lenses.
Your day, your work, your people.

BASTA! is an offline card-drawing app for the Neapolitan (Sicilian) deck — the forty-four cards of the quartara, with the old meanings still attached to them.

You pick a coin, you draw a card, and the card reads the part of the day you asked about. Three draws a day. A reading is a fixed, written text — never generated while you watch — so the same card always says the same thing.

The three lenses

DIAS

The day

The general reading. What the day is actually about.

ACTO

The work

What is in your hands — the job, the thing you were avoiding.

SOCI

The people

Who is near you, and what they want from you.

What it does not do

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, because there is nobody to sign in to.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no in-app purchases.
  • No servers of ours. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no us to send it to.
  • Nothing is generated at runtime. Every reading is written by a person and ships with the app.
  • Public Apple APIs only. No tracking SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics.

There is exactly one network call in the whole app: a fetch of random numbers from the Australian National University’s quantum random number generator, so that a draw is genuinely unpredictable. The app says so on screen, every time. If that fetch fails, the app falls back to your device’s own secure generator.

Who it is for

Two kinds of people. If you own a physical Neapolitan deck, you can look a card up in the app to get its gist back — the old meaning, in plain words, without turning your phone into a slot machine.

And if you would rather not draw at all, you can read the day’s reading for any card, free, without spending one of your three draws. Looking never costs a draw.
